SAMRAT SHARMA — SIMPLE & FULLY EDITABLE WEBSITE

1. Open index.html.
2. Click “Admin / Edit”.
3. Change your name, bio, class, social links, gaming ID, phone, stats, photos and footer.
4. Under “Website Colors”, choose ANY background, accent and text color.
5. Click SAVE, then VIEW WEBSITE.
6. Open Photo Editor for visitor/user photo editing tools.

Your own website photo:
Admin -> Photos -> Hero Photo / About Photo -> Upload -> Save.

Public editing:
Users can use the Photo Editor. A real automatic AI “describe your idea -> generate edit” service needs a hosted AI backend/API; this ZIP does not pretend that a local HTML page is an AI server.
